# References and Source Notes

This package uses public rules/product/research data as engineering constraints, not private CAD.

1. Laws-style badminton racket limits:
   - Overall racket frame length <= 680 mm.
   - Overall width <= 230 mm.
   - Stringed area length <= 280 mm and width <= 220 mm.
   - Stringed area must be a flat crossed-string pattern.

2. Yonex ASTROX 100ZZ product data:
   - 3UG5 and 4UG5 variants.
   - Stringing advice 21–29 lbs.
   - Head-heavy balance.
   - Racquet length 10 mm longer.
   - String pattern 22 × 21.
   - Additional specs: 3U average 88 g.

3. Towler, H.; Mitchell, S. R.; King, M. A. Effects of racket moment of inertia on racket head speed, impact location and shuttlecock speed during the badminton smash. Scientific Reports, 2023.
   - Typical badminton racket mass: 0.085–0.095 kg.
   - Typical swingweight about the 9 cm axis: 90–97 kg cm^2.
   - 9 cm from handle end is a common badminton swingweight axis definition.
   - Fundamental frequency region discussed around 50–55 Hz.

4. Yin, S.-R.; Chang, H.-C.; Cheng, K. B. Impact Characteristics of a Badminton Racket with Realistic Finite Element Modeling. Proceedings, 2020.
   - Commercial racket head length 253 mm and width 188 mm in the string-bed model.
   - String diameter 0.7 mm.
   - Nylon string parameters: Young's modulus 7200 MPa, Poisson ratio 0.3, density 1100 kg/m^3.
   - 26 lbf string tension used as a common player-selected tension.
   - 5 mm displacement impact produced reaction force around 47–48 N.
